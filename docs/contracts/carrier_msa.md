# Carrier Master Services Agreement (summary of key terms)

Applies to: Bluewave Telecom and Cascade Fiber. Sample contract for practice use.

## Section 4.1 Circuit delivery interval

The carrier will deliver and activate each circuit within **30 calendar days** of the order date.
The carrier will send a firm order commitment (FOC) date within 10 business days of the order.

## Section 4.2 Late delivery credits

If a circuit is delivered after the 30-day interval, the customer is owed a credit of
**10% of the one-time installation charge for each full week late**, up to a maximum of 50%.
The one-time installation charge is **$1,500 per circuit**. A circuit 1 to 6 days late earns no credit.
Example: a circuit 15 days late is 2 full weeks late, so the credit is 20% of $1,500, or $300.

## Section 4.3 Claiming credits

The customer must claim credits in writing within **60 days** of the delivery date, listing the
site, order date, due date and actual delivery date. Unclaimed credits are forfeited.

## Section 6.2 Service availability

Each circuit will be available 99.9% of each calendar month, excluding planned maintenance.

## Section 9.1 Escalation contacts

Level 1: carrier account manager. Level 2: carrier regional director, if a circuit is more than
10 days past due or the account manager has not answered within 2 business days.
