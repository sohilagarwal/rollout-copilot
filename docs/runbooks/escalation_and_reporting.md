# Escalation and Reporting Runbook

## When to escalate

| Situation | Escalate to | When |
|---|---|---|
| Circuit past due | Carrier account manager | 1 day past due |
| Circuit past due | Carrier regional director | More than 10 days past due |
| Install vendor first-time success below 90% for the month | Vendor account manager, with data | Same week |
| Hardware backorder that moves a go-live date | Engineering lead, to approve a substitute model | Same day |
| Five or more sites in one wave at risk | VP Operations | At the next weekly review |

## How to write an escalation

1. State the problem in one sentence, with the number of sites affected.
2. List the site IDs and dates.
3. Quote the contract section that applies.
4. Ask for one specific thing with a date, such as new delivery dates by Friday.
5. Keep the tone factual. No blame.

## Weekly status report

Every Monday, one page, for leadership:
1. Overall health: On track, At risk, or Off track, with one sentence why.
2. Numbers: sites live, sites scheduled in the next 3 weeks, sites at risk.
3. Top 3 risks, each with sites affected, cause, owner and next step.
4. Decisions or help needed from leadership.
5. Vendor performance in one line each.
